Email Setup

To send articles to Kindle, configure your email settings in the .env file:

EMAIL_HOST=smtp.gmail.com
EMAIL_PORT=587
EMAIL_USER=your-email@gmail.com
EMAIL_PASS=your-app-password

Also, add your sending email to your Amazon Kindle approved email list at: Amazon Personal Document Settings
